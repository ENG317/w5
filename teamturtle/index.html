Turtle Basics

Turtles are reptiles of the order Testudines. They are characterized by a bony or cartilaginous shell that shields them from predators. Turtles are found in a variety of habitats, including oceans, rivers, and forests.

There are over 300 species of turtles, ranging from small freshwater turtles to large sea turtles. Many species are endangered due to habitat loss, pollution, and poaching.
